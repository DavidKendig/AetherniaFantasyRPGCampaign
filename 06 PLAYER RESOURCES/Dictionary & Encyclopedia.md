###### Numiné
The Numiné are a precursor civilization of Æthernia. Their ruins are scattered all across the countryside and many settlements still retain their ancient Numinén name. No one is sure why the Numiné civilization ceased but the ubiquity of their impact on culture today means they will not be forgotten about anytime soon.
