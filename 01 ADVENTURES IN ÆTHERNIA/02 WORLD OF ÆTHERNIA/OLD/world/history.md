# History & The Veil

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md) | [World Overview](overview.md)

## The Ages of the World

### The Bright Age (Ancient Past)
- Magic was woven into everything — the land, the people, the sky
- Civilizations of immense power existed: human, elven, dwarven, and stranger things
- The world was *alive* with supernatural force
- This age ended in catastrophe

### The Sundering (The Catastrophe)
- Details are lost to time, but the Bright Age ended in a war — or a ritual — or both
- The surviving powers made a choice: to separate the mortal world from the source of magic
- They raised **The Veil** — an invisible, metaphysical barrier that sealed the Otherworld away from the material plane
- Magic didn't disappear overnight, but it *bled out* over generations
- Magical creatures faded, retreated, or died. Enchantments crumbled. Spell-knowledge was forgotten.

### The Dimming (Centuries of Decline)
- Magic became rarer with each generation
- Magical races retreated to hidden enclaves or integrated into human societies, losing their power
- Written records of the Bright Age were gradually lost, destroyed, or locked away
- The Veil also affected *memory* — people collectively forgot, or chose to forget, what the world once was
- The old ruins remain, but no one remembers what they were for

### The Hearth Age (Present Day)
- Magic is functionally absent. The Veil holds strong — or it did.
- The world is mundane, practical, and at peace
- The [Kingdom of Valdris](kingdom-of-valdris.md) is the dominant political power
- Most people would laugh at the idea of real magic
- But the Veil is beginning to [fray](../plot/the-crisis.md)...

## The Veil - What It Actually Is

> **GM Knowledge Only** — Players should NOT know this at campaign start.

The Veil is not a wall. It is more like a frequency — a sustained harmonic that keeps the Otherworld out of phase with the material plane. It was created by the last great mages of the Bright Age as a desperate measure to end the Sundering War.

**Key properties:**
- It is self-sustaining but not eternal. It was always going to weaken eventually.
- It is anchored to **seven physical locations** in the Hearthlands — the Veilstones. These appear to be natural rock formations to the untrained eye.
- It suppresses magical manifestation. Even latent magical talent in people is dampened.
- It subtly influences collective psychology — making people incurious about magic, comfortable with the mundane.

**What [Seraine](../npcs/seraine-duskhollow.md) discovered:**
- The Pale Liturgy — an ancient text — describes the Veil's architecture
- It contains the locations of the Veilstones
- It describes a ritual to *unmake* each anchor, weakening the Veil progressively
- Seraine has already destroyed one Veilstone. The effects are beginning to show.

## The Otherworld

The realm sealed away by the Veil. Not heaven, not hell — something stranger. A place of raw creative and destructive magical force. Spirits, entities, and energies that once coexisted with the mortal world.

It is not inherently evil. But it is *wild*, and its sudden return to a world that has forgotten magic will be catastrophic. Like a dam breaking.
