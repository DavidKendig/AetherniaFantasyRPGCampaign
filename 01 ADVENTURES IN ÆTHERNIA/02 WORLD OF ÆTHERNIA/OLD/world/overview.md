# The Hearthlands - World Overview

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md)

## The Known World

The Hearthlands is a broad stretch of temperate land — rolling farmland, ancient forests, cold northern highlands, and a rocky western coast. It is a world that feels *old* but *quiet*. The great empires are dust. The dragons (if they ever existed) are gone. Magic is a fireside story told to children, and most people live their entire lives without encountering anything they can't explain.

This is deliberate. This is the Veil's doing — though almost no one alive knows it.

## The Feel of the World

**Early Campaign (Low Fantasy):**
- Magic is essentially absent from daily life
- Monsters are rare — wolves, bandits, and disease are the real threats
- Superstition exists, but most "magic" is herbalism, folk remedies, and coincidence
- The world feels like late medieval Europe — muddy roads, feudal lords, trade guilds
- Weapons are iron and steel. Armor is practical. There are no enchanted swords.

**Mid Campaign (Transition):**
- Strange events become harder to explain away
- Animals behave unnaturally. Crops grow in impossible patterns. People dream the same dreams.
- Old ruins begin to hum, glow, or shift
- Individuals begin manifesting abilities they cannot control
- The world is *waking up* and it is terrifying

**Late Campaign (High Fantasy):**
- Magic floods back into the world as the [Veil](history.md) weakens
- Creatures of myth return — or emerge for the first time
- The landscape itself changes: floating stones, wild magic zones, spirit-touched forests
- Players must navigate a world that has fundamentally transformed

## Geography (Broad Strokes)

| Region | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **The Greenway** | Central farmland belt. Breadbasket of Valdris. Where [Millhaven](../locations/millhaven.md) sits. |
| **The Thornwood** | Dense, ancient forest to the east. Locals avoid the deep interior. |
| **The Greymere** | Major river system. Trade artery connecting inland towns to the coast. |
| **The Ashfall Peaks** | Northern mountain range. Old mines, older ruins. |
| **The Saltwaste Coast** | Western shoreline. Fishing villages, port towns, fog. |
| **Valdris City** | The capital. Seat of the crown. See [Kingdom of Valdris](kingdom-of-valdris.md). |
