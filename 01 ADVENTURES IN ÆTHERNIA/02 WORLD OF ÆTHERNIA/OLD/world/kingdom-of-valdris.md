# The Kingdom of Valdris

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md) | [World Overview](overview.md)

## Overview

Valdris is the primary human kingdom of the Hearthlands. It is not an empire — it is a single, large feudal state held together by tradition, trade, and a mostly-competent monarchy. Think of it as a kingdom that has enjoyed peace long enough to become complacent.

## Government

**Ruler:** King Aldric Valdris IV — A pragmatic, aging king more concerned with harvests and tax collection than glory. He is not cruel, but he is not inspiring either. He trusts his advisors, perhaps too much.

**Structure:**
- The Crown sits in **Valdris City**
- The kingdom is divided into **Marches** — each governed by a **Margrave** who answers to the king
- Local towns and villages are run by **Reeves** (appointed administrators) or **Elders** (in smaller settlements)
- There is no standing magical authority because there is no recognized magic

**The Royal Archive:** A vast library in Valdris City. Once the seat of magical scholarship in the old empire. Now it houses dusty histories, tax records, and — buried deep in restricted vaults — texts that predate the Veil. This is where [Seraine Duskhollow](../npcs/seraine-duskhollow.md) found the Pale Liturgy.

## Military

- **The Crown Guard:** Professional soldiers stationed in Valdris City and major towns
- **March Wardens:** Regional militia forces under each Margrave
- **No magical corps.** No war-mages, no enchanted siege weapons. War is steel, shields, and arrows.

## Economy

- Agriculture-based. Grain, livestock, timber, and iron are the backbone.
- Trade flows along the Greymere River and the Greenway Road
- Guilds hold significant economic power in larger towns
- The [Wayfarers' Compact](../factions/wayfarers-compact.md) operates legally under a royal charter

## Culture & Religion

- **The Hearthkeeper Faith** — A quiet, practical religion. Worships no named gods, but honors "the Hearth" as a symbol of home, community, and endurance. Clergy are more like community organizers than priests. No miracles.
- Superstition about the old world exists but is considered backward. Educated people dismiss it.
- There is a cultural *aversion* to magic — not fear exactly, but a deep-seated belief that the world is better without it. This is an echo of the Veil's influence on collective memory.

## Current State

Valdris is stable but stagnant. The kingdom has not faced a real existential threat in living memory. The Crown Guard is under-trained. The Margraves bicker over trade rights. The people are content but not prepared for what is coming.

The first signs of [the Fraying](../plot/the-crisis.md) will be dismissed as local oddities. By the time the Crown takes notice, it may be too late.
