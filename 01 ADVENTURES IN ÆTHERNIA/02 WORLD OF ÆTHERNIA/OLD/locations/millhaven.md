# Millhaven - Starting Town

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md) | [Key Locations](key-locations.md)

## Overview

Millhaven is a quiet trade town on the banks of the Greymere River, deep in the Greenway — the agricultural heartland of [Valdris](../world/kingdom-of-valdris.md). It is exactly the kind of place where nothing interesting happens. That's why it's the perfect place for interesting things to start happening.

**Population:** ~800
**Notable Feature:** The Greymere Mill — a large waterwheel-driven grain mill that serves the surrounding farmsteads
**Governance:** Reeve Colm Ashford, appointed by the Margrave of the Central Greenway

## Why Millhaven?

- It is small enough that the players are important, but large enough to have an inn, a market, and problems
- It sits on a trade route, so travelers (and trouble) pass through regularly
- There are old ruins in the nearby Thornwood that locals avoid — perfect for early dungeon crawls
- The [Wayfarers' Compact](../factions/wayfarers-compact.md) has a small posting board here

## Key Locations in Town

### The Greymere Mill
The economic heart of the town. Farmers from a dozen surrounding homesteads bring grain here. Run by **Miller Hesta Dunwood**, a no-nonsense widow who knows everyone's business. The mill is where Session 1 begins — something is wrong with the grain.

### The Axle & Spoke Inn
The only inn in town. Run by **Brennan Cask**, a retired caravan guard with a bad knee and a good memory for faces. Two stories, eight rooms, decent stew. The common room is where locals gossip, travelers rest, and the Compact's posting board hangs.

### The Posting Board
A weathered wooden board nailed to the wall of the Axle & Spoke. [Wayfarers' Compact](../factions/wayfarers-compact.md) contracts are pinned here alongside local notices. This is how the players find work and get drawn into the plot.

### Ashford House
Home and office of **Reeve Colm Ashford**. A harried administrator who just wants the taxes collected on time and no trouble from the Margrave. He will hire the players reluctantly when problems exceed what the town watch (four men) can handle.

### Maren's Apothecary
Run by **Maren Blackthorn**, an herbalist with a sharp tongue and a surprising depth of knowledge about old folklore. She is the closest thing to a "wise woman" in town. She does *not* practice magic — but she knows more about the old stories than she lets on. A key NPC for drip-feeding lore.

### The Chapel of the Hearth
A simple stone building where the local **Hearthkeeper**, Brother Aldwin, tends to the community's spiritual needs. Weddings, funerals, harvest blessings. He is kind but utterly unprepared for anything supernatural.

### The Old Stone Bridge
South of town, a bridge crosses the Greymere. It is older than the town — much older. The stones are fitted without mortar in a style no local mason can replicate. Locals don't think much of it. It's a Bright Age relic.

## Surrounding Area

- **Farmsteads:** Dozens of small farms within a half-day's ride
- **The Thornwood Edge:** The ancient forest begins about two miles east of town. Locals harvest timber from the edges but do not go deep. Old ruins lie within.
- **The Greymere River:** Flows north to south. Trade barges travel it regularly.
- **The Greenway Road:** Packed-earth trade road running east-west through town

## The Mood

Millhaven should feel *safe* — at first. Cozy, even. The players should settle in, meet the locals, take a few odd jobs. Then the wrongness starts to creep in at the edges. A smell in the mill. A sound from the bridge at night. A farmer's cow born with too many eyes.

The town itself is not in danger — yet. But it is the players' home base, and watching it slowly change as the [Fraying](../plot/the-crisis.md) progresses should be deeply unsettling.
