## Locations by Region

### The Greenway (Central Farmlands)
| Location | Type | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| [Millhaven](millhaven.md) | Starting Town | Trade town on the Greymere. Players' home base. |
| Dunmore | Market Town | Larger than Millhaven. Margrave's seat for the Central Greenway. Two days' ride west. |
| The Greenway Road | Trade Route | Main east-west artery through the farmlands. |

### The Thornwood (Eastern Forest)
| Location | Type | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| The Thornwood Edge | Wilderness | Harvested for timber. Safe enough by daylight. |
| The Deep Thornwood | Wilderness | Locals avoid it. Old growth. Strange sounds. Animals behave oddly. |
| The Pale Stones | Ruin | A ring of white standing stones in a clearing, three hours into the deep wood. **One of the seven Veilstones.** Players will discover this in mid-campaign. |

### The Greymere River
| Location | Type | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Greymere Ford | Crossing | Shallow crossing point north of Millhaven. |
| Bargewright Landing | River Port | Southern trade hub where the Greymere meets the coast road. |

### The Ashfall Peaks (Northern Mountains)
| Location | Type | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Ironhold | Mining Town | Iron and tin mines. Hard people, hard winters. |
| The Hollows | Ruin/Dungeon | Abandoned mine complex that extends far deeper than any natural mine should. Pre-dates Valdris. |

### Valdris City (The Capital)
| Location | Type | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| The Royal Archive | Library/Vault | Where Seraine found the Pale Liturgy. Restricted lower levels. |
| The Crown Keep | Castle | Seat of King Aldric. |
| The Wayfarer Hall | Guild HQ | Central headquarters of the [Wayfarers' Compact](../factions/wayfarers-compact.md). |

### The Saltwaste Coast (Western Shore)
| Location | Type | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Greymouth | Port City | Largest coastal settlement. Fog-shrouded. Smuggler presence. |
| The Drowned Stairs | Ruin | Stone steps descending into the sea at low tide. Origin unknown. |

## Locations of Campaign Importance

> **GM Eyes Only**

The seven **Veilstones** are scattered across the Hearthlands. [Seraine](../npcs/seraine-duskhollow.md) has already destroyed the first (located in the Ashfall Peaks — The Hollows). She is working toward the second. The locations are:

1. ~~The Hollows, Ashfall Peaks~~ — **Destroyed**
2. The Pale Stones, Deep Thornwood — Near Millhaven. This will become relevant in sessions 4-6.
3. The Drowned Stairs, Saltwaste Coast
4. Beneath the Royal Archive, Valdris City
5. Three more in distant/unexplored regions (to be developed later)
