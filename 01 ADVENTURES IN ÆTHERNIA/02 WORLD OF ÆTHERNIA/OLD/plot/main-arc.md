# The Main Arc

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md)

## Campaign Structure

The campaign follows a five-act structure, though the boundaries between acts are fluid. Only Act 1 is detailed here. The rest are sketched as direction, not prescription — the players will shape how the story unfolds.

---

## Act 1: Strange Days (Sessions 1-8)

**Tone:** Low fantasy. Mystery. Creeping unease.

**What the players experience:**
- Odd jobs in and around [Millhaven](../locations/millhaven.md) through the [Wayfarers' Compact](../factions/wayfarers-compact.md)
- Increasingly strange events that defy mundane explanation
- Local NPCs reacting with denial, fear, or fascination
- The first hints that these events are connected — and not natural

**What is actually happening:**
- The first [Veilstone](../world/history.md) was destroyed six months ago. Its effects are radiating outward.
- [Seraine](../npcs/seraine-duskhollow.md) is searching for the second Veilstone.
- The Veil is weakening in the region. Magic is seeping back in — raw, uncontrolled, and wrong.

**Act 1 ends when:** The players discover that the strange events have a single cause, encounter evidence of the Ashen Court, or find something that explicitly points to the Veil's existence.

---

## Act 2: The Unraveling (Sessions 9-15) *[Sketch]*

**Tone:** Transitional. The world is changing and everyone can see it now.

- Magic manifestations become undeniable and public
- The Compact and the Crown must respond — poorly, at first
- Players encounter Seraine's agents directly
- A second Veilstone is destroyed (or the players prevent it)
- Players begin to understand the Veil and the stakes
- First contact with something from the Otherworld

---

## Act 3: The New World (Sessions 16-24) *[Sketch]*

**Tone:** High fantasy emerges. Wonder and terror in equal measure.

- The world has fundamentally changed. Magic is real and everywhere.
- Players must navigate new power structures, magical factions, and transformed landscapes
- Seraine steps into the open — and she is now powerful
- The question becomes: stop the Fraying, manage it, or embrace it?
- Old alliances fracture. New ones form.

---

## Act 4: The Choice (Sessions 25-32) *[Sketch]*

**Tone:** High fantasy. Epic scope.

- The remaining Veilstones are contested
- The Otherworld begins to bleed through in dangerous ways
- Players must decide what kind of world they want to live in
- Seraine may be an enemy, a reluctant ally, or something else
- The campaign builds toward a climax at the final Veilstone

---

## Act 5: The New Age (Sessions 33+) *[Sketch]*

**Tone:** Resolution. The world will never be the same.

- The Veil is either restored, destroyed, or something in between
- The consequences of the players' choices play out
- A new equilibrium — or a new crisis — emerges
- Epilogue sessions to explore what the world becomes

---

## Recurring Themes

1. **Knowledge vs. Ignorance** — Is it better to know the truth, even if it's dangerous?
2. **Change vs. Preservation** — The world was safe but diminished. Is that acceptable?
3. **Individual vs. System** — Seraine is one person against a world order. Is she a revolutionary or a destroyer?
4. **The Mundane Has Value** — The low-fantasy world the players start in should feel worth protecting. Millhaven and its people matter.
