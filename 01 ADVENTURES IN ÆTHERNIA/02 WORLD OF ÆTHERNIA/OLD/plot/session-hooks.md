# Early Session Hooks

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md) | [Main Arc](main-arc.md)

## Overview

These hooks are designed to feel like ordinary low-fantasy work at first, with each one carrying a thread that connects to the larger [Fraying](the-crisis.md) crisis. The players shouldn't realize they're on the main quest yet — they're just solving local problems that happen to be symptoms of something much bigger.

## Compact Posting Board Jobs

These can appear on the [Millhaven posting board](../locations/millhaven.md) at any time and serve as side content between main session plots.

### Available at Campaign Start
| Contract | Pay | Description |
|----------|-----|-------------|
| Wolf Trouble | 8 silver | A farmstead north of town reports a wolf pack that won't disperse. The wolves aren't hunting — they're watching the farmhouse. (Fraying: animals behaving unnaturally) |
| Missing Merchant | 12 silver | A peddler named Vic Harren is three days overdue from the Greenway Road. Find him or his goods. (Red herring — he got drunk in another town. But the road is stranger than it should be.) |
| Cellar Clearance | 5 silver | Something is nesting in old Garret's root cellar. Probably rats. (It is rats — but they've built something unsettlingly deliberate down there. Geometric patterns in the dirt.) |
| Thornwood Timber | 10 silver | Woodcutters refuse to enter their usual Thornwood cutting site. Investigate and clear the area. (Nothing visibly dangerous — but the silence is *wrong*. No birds, no insects. Trees have faint markings.) |

### Available After Session 2
| Contract | Pay | Description |
|----------|-----|-------------|
| The Sleepless | 15 silver | Dalla Voss posts this personally. Three farmsteads are reporting the same symptoms: nightmares, sleepwalking, residents waking with dirt under their fingernails. Investigate. |
| Bridge Watch | 6 silver | Reeve Ashford wants someone to watch the Old Stone Bridge at night. Travelers report hearing something. (They'll hear — and feel — the hum.) |

### Available After Session 3
| Contract | Pay | Description |
|----------|-----|-------------|
| The Bloom | 20 silver | A dead farmer's field erupted overnight with impossible flowers. Dalla Voss wants it documented and samples collected. Maren Blackthorn should examine them. |

## Rumors & Gossip (Axle & Spoke Common Room)

Roll or choose when players spend an evening at the inn:

1. "My cousin up in Ironhold says they sealed off one of the old mines. Won't say why." *(The Hollows — site of the first destroyed Veilstone)*
2. "Bargewright Landing hasn't sent a shipment in two weeks. River's fine. They just... stopped." *(Unrelated — labor dispute. Not everything is the Fraying.)*
3. "Maren Blackthorn was seen at the Old Stone Bridge at dawn three days running. Just standing there." *(She senses something. She doesn't know what.)*
4. "A Royal Archivist went missing a few years back. Took some books with her. Crown put out a quiet search, then dropped it." *(Seraine. This is a very early breadcrumb.)*
5. "The Thornwood is older than Valdris. Older than anything. My grandmother said there are stones in there that sing." *(The Pale Stones — the second Veilstone.)*
6. "Farmer Dunnick's cow birthed a calf with three eyes last week. He killed it and won't talk about it." *(Tier 1 Fraying)*
