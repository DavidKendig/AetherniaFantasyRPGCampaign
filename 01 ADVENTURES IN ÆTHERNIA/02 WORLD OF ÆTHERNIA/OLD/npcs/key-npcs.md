## Millhaven NPCs

These are the characters the players will interact with most in the early campaign.

### Reeve Colm Ashford
- **Role:** Town administrator
- **Personality:** Harried, cautious, wants no trouble. Answers to the Margrave in Dunmore.
- **Useful for:** Official authority, paying for jobs, providing local context
- **Secret:** None. He's exactly what he appears to be — an overwhelmed bureaucrat.

### Hesta Dunwood (Miller)
- **Role:** Owner/operator of the Greymere Mill
- **Personality:** No-nonsense, sharp-eyed, widowed. Knows every farmer in the region. Tough but fair.
- **Useful for:** Local gossip, practical knowledge, the inciting incident of Session 1
- **Secret:** Her late husband once found something strange in the mill's foundation stones — old carvings he couldn't explain. She dismissed it then. She'll remember when things get strange.

### Brennan Cask (Innkeeper)
- **Role:** Owner of the Axle & Spoke Inn
- **Personality:** Warm, talkative, retired caravan guard. Bad left knee. Collects stories from travelers.
- **Useful for:** Rumors, introductions, a safe base of operations. Manages the Compact posting board.
- **Secret:** He once traveled as far as the Ashfall Peaks in his guard days. He saw something in the mountains he's never spoken about. (This becomes relevant when the Ashfall Peaks plotline develops.)

### Maren Blackthorn (Herbalist)
- **Role:** Apothecary and herbalist
- **Personality:** Sharp-tongued, intelligent, reclusive. Lives at the edge of town near the Thornwood.
- **Useful for:** Healing, herbal remedies, old folklore and lore drops. She is the GM's primary tool for feeding players information about the old world.
- **Secret:** Maren has a collection of old books inherited from her grandmother — journals that describe things that shouldn't exist. She has always assumed they were fiction. She's starting to wonder.
- **Arc:** As the Fraying progresses, Maren becomes increasingly important. She may be one of the first people to manifest latent magical talent.

### Brother Aldwin (Hearthkeeper)
- **Role:** Local clergyman of the Hearthkeeper Faith
- **Personality:** Gentle, community-minded, deeply kind. Utterly out of his depth with anything supernatural.
- **Useful for:** Community trust, moral guidance, a voice of normalcy
- **Secret:** None — but his faith will be tested when the world changes. How he responds is a story thread.

## Regional NPCs

### Dalla Voss (Pathfinder, Wayfarers' Compact)
- **Role:** The nearest [Compact](../factions/wayfarers-compact.md) authority, based in Dunmore
- **Personality:** Professional, fair, skeptical. Mid-30s. Former soldier. Takes her duties seriously.
- **Useful for:** Contract assignments, Compact advancement, wider-world news
- **Visits Millhaven:** Every 2-3 weeks. Players can meet her in Session 2 or 3.
- **Arc:** Dalla is the players' main connection to the wider Compact. As strange contracts increase, she becomes an ally — or a skeptic who needs convincing.

### Warden Theron Gale
- **Role:** Compact Warden, runs the Dunmore chapter house
- **Personality:** Politically careful, ambitious within the Compact. Not corrupt, but prioritizes stability over truth.
- **Useful for:** Higher-level Compact politics, resources, and eventually the decision of whether the Compact takes the Fraying seriously
- **Not met until:** Sessions 5+

## Villain NPCs

### [Seraine Duskhollow](seraine-duskhollow.md)
The main villain. Former Royal Archivist. See her dedicated file.

### Oren Dace (Ashen Court Enforcer)
- **Role:** Seraine's field operative and protector
- **Personality:** Quiet, intense, haunted. Former soldier. Does not enjoy violence but is very good at it.
- **Motivation:** He saw the aftermath of the Veilstone destruction in the Hollows. It broke something in him. Seraine put him back together. He owes her everything.
- **Not encountered until:** Sessions 4-6 (scouting near Millhaven)

### Ysmay (Ashen Court Disciple)
- **Role:** Seraine's protege and emerging seer
- **Personality:** Young (early 20s), earnest, frightened by her own visions, desperate to understand them
- **Motivation:** The Fraying gave her visions she can't control. Seraine is the only person who can explain what's happening to her.
- **Not encountered until:** Sessions 8+
