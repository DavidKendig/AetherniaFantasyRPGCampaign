# Seraine Duskhollow - Main Villain

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md) | [Key NPCs](key-npcs.md)

## The Short Version

Seraine is not evil. She is *wrong*. That's what makes her dangerous.

## Background

**Age:** 43
**Former Title:** Senior Archivist of the Royal Archive, Valdris City
**Current Status:** Officially listed as "resigned and departed." In truth, she is operating in hiding across the Hearthlands.

Seraine spent twenty years cataloging the oldest texts in the Royal Archive. She was brilliant, meticulous, and increasingly obsessed with the inconsistencies she found — references to an older world, a brighter world, one that didn't match the history anyone taught. She began to suspect that something fundamental had been *taken* from the world.

Five years ago, she found the **Pale Liturgy** — a text hidden in a sealed vault that hadn't been opened in centuries. It described the [Veil](../world/history.md), the Veilstones, and the ritual to unmake them. It also described the world before: a world of wonder, of magic woven into every living thing, of connection to the Otherworld.

Seraine didn't snap. She didn't go mad. She simply became *convinced* — with the patient certainty of a scholar who has found proof — that the Veil was a crime against the natural order. That humanity had been *diminished* by it. That the world deserved to be whole again.

She resigned from the Archive, took the Liturgy, and disappeared.

## Motivation

Seraine genuinely believes she is saving the world. She sees the Veil as a cage that has kept humanity small, mortal, and blind. She wants to restore the Bright Age — to return magic, wonder, and connection to the world.

**What she doesn't fully understand:**
- The Veil was raised for a *reason*. The Bright Age ended in catastrophe.
- Removing the Veil all at once will not gently restore magic — it will *flood* the world with uncontrolled supernatural force
- The Otherworld has changed in the centuries of separation. What comes through may not be what she expects.
- People are not prepared. The transition will cause suffering, madness, and death on a massive scale.

She knows there will be a cost. She believes it is worth paying. She is wrong about how high that cost will be.

## Personality

- **Patient.** She has a multi-year plan and is in no rush.
- **Empathetic.** She is not cruel. She takes no pleasure in collateral damage. She recruits through compassion, not coercion.
- **Absolutely certain.** This is her fatal flaw. She cannot be argued out of her position because she has *studied* this for decades. She has the evidence. She is right — except about the parts she can't see.
- **Charismatic in a quiet way.** She doesn't give speeches. She sits with people, listens, explains, and makes them feel understood.

## Abilities

At campaign start, Seraine has **no magical abilities**. She is a scholar, not a mage. She enacts the rituals from the Pale Liturgy through precise, researched steps — not personal power.

As the Veil weakens, she *will* begin to manifest abilities. She is one of the first to be touched by the returning magic. By mid-campaign, she is becoming something more than human. By late campaign, she may be the most powerful being in the Hearthlands.

## Current Activities (Session 1-3 Timeframe)

- She destroyed the first Veilstone in the Ashfall Peaks approximately six months before the campaign begins
- She is currently researching the location of the second Veilstone (the Pale Stones in the Thornwood, near [Millhaven](../locations/millhaven.md) — though she doesn't know the exact location yet)
- She is nowhere near Millhaven at this point
- The strange events the players encounter are *aftershocks* of the first Veilstone's destruction, not her direct actions

## How Players Encounter Her

| Phase | Contact |
|-------|---------|
| Sessions 1-3 | They don't. They encounter effects, not the cause. |
| Sessions 4-8 | Her agents appear. Her name may be found in documents or mentioned by NPCs. |
| Sessions 9-15 | The players may meet her — and she will be reasonable, articulate, and hard to simply dismiss as a villain. |
| Sessions 16+ | She has become powerful. Negotiation or confrontation — the players' choice. |

## The Moral Question

Seraine's plan raises questions the players will have to wrestle with:
- Was the Veil the right choice? Should magic have been sealed away?
- Is a world without magic truly lesser?
- If the Veil is going to fail eventually anyway, is Seraine just accelerating the inevitable?
- Can the transition be managed, or must it be stopped entirely?

There should be no easy answer.
