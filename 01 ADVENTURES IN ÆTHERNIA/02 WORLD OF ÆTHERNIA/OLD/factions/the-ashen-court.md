# The Ashen Court - Villain Faction

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md)

## Overview

The Ashen Court is not a faction the players will hear about for a long time. It does not have a name that its members use openly. "The Ashen Court" is the GM's label for the growing network of agents, converts, and unwitting pawns serving [Seraine Duskhollow's](../npcs/seraine-duskhollow.md) mission to unmake the [Veil](../world/history.md).

In the early campaign, the Ashen Court barely exists. It is Seraine and a handful of followers. As the campaign progresses, it grows — attracting those who welcome magic's return, those who seek power, and those who have been broken by the Fraying and are desperate for someone who seems to understand what is happening.

## Structure (Such As It Is)

**Leader:** [Seraine Duskhollow](../npcs/seraine-duskhollow.md) — the architect and sole person who understands the full plan.

**Inner Circle (2-3 people at campaign start):**
- **Oren Dace** — A former Crown Guard sergeant who deserted after stumbling onto something in the Ashfall Peaks. Seraine found him half-mad and gave him purpose. He is her enforcer and field operative. Dangerous, loyal, haunted.
- **Ysmay** — No surname. A young woman from the Saltwaste Coast who began having visions after the first Veilstone was destroyed. Seraine is grooming her as a disciple. Ysmay genuinely believes the Veil's removal will heal the world.

**Outer Ring (unwitting or loosely connected):**
- Scholars who received anonymous letters pointing them toward forbidden texts
- Desperate people in areas most affected by the Fraying, who have been offered "explanations" and "protection"
- A few corrupt officials who have been bribed or blackmailed

## Methods

The Ashen Court operates through:
- **Secrecy:** No one outside the inner circle knows Seraine's identity or full plan
- **Misdirection:** Problems caused by Veilstone destruction are blamed on natural causes, local corruption, or old curses
- **Recruitment through desperation:** As the Fraying causes more chaos, more people become willing to listen to someone who has answers
- **Archaeological recovery:** Seraine needs information from old ruins to locate remaining Veilstones. She sends agents to explore and retrieve artifacts/texts.

## Presence Near Millhaven

At campaign start, the Ashen Court has **no direct presence** near Millhaven. However:
- The effects of the first destroyed Veilstone (in the Ashfall Peaks) are rippling outward
- Oren Dace may pass through the region in sessions 4-6, scouting the Pale Stones in the Thornwood
- Players will not encounter named Ashen Court agents in the first three sessions — only the *consequences* of their actions

## Campaign Progression

| Phase | Ashen Court Activity |
|-------|---------------------|
| Sessions 1-3 | Invisible. Players deal with symptoms, not the cause. |
| Sessions 4-8 | First clues. An agent is encountered. A name is overheard. |
| Sessions 9-15 | The Court is identified as a threat. Players begin to oppose them directly. |
| Sessions 16+ | Open conflict. The Court has grown. Seraine steps out of the shadows. |
