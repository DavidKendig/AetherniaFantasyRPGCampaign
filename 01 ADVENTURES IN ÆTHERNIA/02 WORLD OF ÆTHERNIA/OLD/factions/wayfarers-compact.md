# The Wayfarers' Compact - Adventurer's Guild

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md)

## Overview

The Wayfarers' Compact is not a guild of heroes. It is a licensed organization of problem-solvers, sellswords, escorts, scouts, and investigators operating under a royal charter from the Crown of [Valdris](../world/kingdom-of-valdris.md). Think of it as a cross between a mercenary company, a private investigation firm, and a labor union.

In a low-fantasy world without monsters to slay or dungeons to delve, the Compact exists because the Crown Guard can't be everywhere, and local militia can't handle everything. Someone has to deal with the wolf pack harassing the northern farms, the bandits on the Greenway Road, or the merchant whose cargo keeps vanishing.

## Structure

**Headquarters:** Wayfarer Hall, Valdris City
**Chapter Houses:** In major towns (Dunmore, Greymouth, Ironhold)
**Posting Boards:** In smaller towns and inns — including [Millhaven](../locations/millhaven.md)

### Ranks
| Rank | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Petitioner** | Unregistered. Can take posting board jobs but has no official backing. |
| **Wayfarer** | Registered member. Carries a Compact seal. Can take contracts, has legal protections. |
| **Pathfinder** | Senior member. Can issue contracts, mentor Wayfarers, represent the Compact officially. |
| **Warden** | Regional authority. Runs a chapter house or oversees a territory. |
| **The Accord** | Ruling council of five Wardens in Valdris City. Rarely seen. |

### How It Works
1. **Contracts** are posted on boards or issued directly by Pathfinders/Wardens
2. Wayfarers select contracts and report completion for payment
3. The Compact takes a **15% cut** of all contract payments
4. Members are expected to follow the **Compact Code**: no theft from clients, no murder for hire, no work against the Crown
5. The Compact provides: legal standing (Wayfarers cannot be arrested for carrying weapons in towns), access to chapter house resources, and a network of contacts

## The Compact in Millhaven

There is no chapter house in Millhaven — it's too small. But there *is* a posting board at the [Axle & Spoke Inn](../locations/millhaven.md). Contracts are delivered by river barge or by traveling Pathfinders.

**Local Pathfinder:** None permanently assigned. The nearest Pathfinder is **Dalla Voss**, based in Dunmore. She visits Millhaven every few weeks to check the board and collect reports.

## Joining the Compact

Players can join as Petitioners immediately by simply taking jobs off the posting board. To become full Wayfarers, they need:
- Completion of three verified contracts
- A sponsor (a Pathfinder or higher who vouches for them)
- Payment of a 5-silver registration fee

This gives the players a natural early-game progression track and a reason to take odd jobs.

## Role in the Campaign

**Early game:** The Compact is the players' employer and structure. It gives them reasons to investigate problems, travel, and get involved in local affairs.

**Mid game:** As [the Fraying](../plot/the-crisis.md) escalates, the Compact becomes overwhelmed. Strange contracts flood in — things no one knows how to deal with. The organization starts to fracture between those who want to investigate the supernatural events and those who want to pretend everything is normal.

**Late game:** The Compact could become the backbone of a resistance against the chaos, or it could collapse. Depends on what the players do.

## Key Compact NPCs
- **Dalla Voss** — Nearest Pathfinder. Practical, fair, skeptical. See [Key NPCs](../npcs/key-npcs.md).
- **Warden Theron Gale** — Runs the Dunmore chapter house. Politically cautious. Will become important when the crisis can no longer be ignored.
