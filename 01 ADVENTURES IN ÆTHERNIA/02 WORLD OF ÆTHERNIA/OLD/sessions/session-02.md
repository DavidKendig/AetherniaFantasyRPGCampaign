# Session 2: The Fever Dream

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md) | [Previous: Session 1](session-01.md) | [Next: Session 3](session-03.md)

## Session Overview

**Goal:** Escalate the unease. The strange events are no longer isolated — they're a pattern. Introduce the shared nightmares and deepen the mystery.

**Tone:** Unsettling. The mundane world is starting to feel thin.

**Estimated Length:** 3-4 hours

---

## Part 1: Morning After

### The Dreams
Open the session with descriptions of the players' dreams from the previous night (if they slept in Millhaven). Each player has a variation of the same dream:

> *You are underground. The air is warm and thick, like breath. Stone walls stretch in every direction — smooth, carved, impossibly old. There is a light ahead, pale and steady, like moonlight trapped in glass. You walk toward it. It grows brighter but never closer. You hear a sound — not a voice, not music — something between the two. It fills your chest. You feel like the ground beneath you is not stone but skin, and something beneath it is breathing.*

> *You wake up. Your fingernails are dirty.*

### Town Reaction
As players move through town, they learn they weren't alone:
- **Brennan Cask** slept badly. He won't say more — he's embarrassed.
- **Three farmsteads** north of town report the same: nightmares, sleepwalking, people waking with dirt under their nails as if they'd been digging.
- **Hesta Dunwood** didn't dream but her two mill workers did. One refuses to come to work.
- **Brother Aldwin** is conducting a blessing at the chapel. Attendance is unusually high.

### Maren's Discovery
If the players visited [Maren Blackthorn](../npcs/key-npcs.md) in Session 1, she sends word that she's found something. If they didn't, she comes to the inn looking for "the people who investigated the mill."

**What Maren shares:**
- The carvings from the mill foundation match symbols in her grandmother's journals
- Her grandmother called them "the old script" and believed they were markers — like boundary stones
- The journals mention that the old builders placed these markers "where the world is thin"
- Maren is visibly shaken. She has always dismissed the journals as fantasy. The mill carvings are making her reconsider.
- She asks the players to be discreet. She doesn't want to be branded a superstitious fool.

**What Maren doesn't know:** What "thin" means, who the old builders were, or why this is happening now. She is a resource, not an oracle.

## Part 2: The Sleepwalkers

### The Contract
[Dalla Voss](../npcs/key-npcs.md) arrives in Millhaven — either on her scheduled visit or drawn by reports. She is practical and concerned. She posts the **Sleepless** contract (15 silver):

> Three farmsteads on the north road are reporting identical symptoms: shared nightmares, sleepwalking, residents waking with dirt under their fingernails. Investigate. Determine if this is illness, contamination, or something else. Report findings.

### Investigation

**The Farmsteads:**
The three affected farmsteads are within a mile of each other on the north road. All three families report:
- Dreams started four nights ago (two nights before the mill rot was discovered)
- Every person in the household has the same dream
- Sleepwalkers head in the same direction: northeast, toward the Thornwood
- One farmer, **old Marek Dunnick**, followed his sleepwalking wife. She walked a quarter mile toward the forest edge before he woke her. She was digging at the ground with her bare hands.

**What players find at the digging site:**
- A shallow depression where Marek's wife was digging
- Beneath a foot of topsoil: another warm stone, another set of old carvings
- The carvings are the same style as the mill foundation
- The stone is part of something larger — a buried structure that extends toward the Thornwood
- The warmth pulses faintly, like a slow heartbeat

**The pattern:** The mill foundation, this buried stone, and (if players think to check) the Old Stone Bridge south of town — they form a rough line pointing into the Thornwood. Toward something.

### Dalla's Reaction
When the players report:
- If they mention only the nightmares and sleepwalking: Dalla is concerned but practical. She'll recommend consulting a physician in Dunmore.
- If they mention the warm stones and carvings: Dalla is skeptical but fair. She says she'll mention it in her report to [Warden Gale](../npcs/key-npcs.md) but advises against "jumping to conclusions."
- If they press the supernatural angle: "I don't deal in ghost stories. But I deal in patterns, and this is a pattern. Keep investigating. Document everything."

## Part 3: The Night Watch (Optional Encounter)

If players investigate the Old Stone Bridge at night:

- The bridge hums. It is felt in the chest more than heard — a deep vibration, rhythmic, like something massive breathing far underground.
- The river water near the bridge flows slightly *wrong* — eddying in unnatural patterns, occasionally seeming to flow upstream for a heartbeat.
- If players touch the bridge stones: warmth, and a flash of sensation — the same pale light from the dreams, but waking. It lasts a heartbeat and is gone.
- Nothing attacks them. Nothing appears. It is just *wrong*. And it is getting stronger.

### Optional Combat Encounter
If the table needs action, a pack of wolves has gathered near the bridge. They are not hostile — they are staring at the bridge, silent and still. If the players approach aggressively, the wolves scatter. If they approach carefully, they can observe: the wolves are arranged in a rough semicircle, all facing the same point. Their eyes reflect the moonlight oddly — too bright, too steady.

This is animal behavior responding to the Fraying. The wolves are not a threat — they are a sign.

---

## End of Session

### Status
- The players have identified a pattern: the mill, the buried stone, the bridge, all in a line toward the Thornwood
- Maren has old knowledge that connects to what they're finding
- Dalla is aware but not convinced
- The town is uneasy but not panicking
- The dreams continue

### Hook for Session 3
Maren approaches the players privately. She's been reading more of her grandmother's journals. There's a reference to "the pale stones in the deep wood" — a place her grandmother warned about but never visited. The line of warm stones points directly at the Thornwood interior.

She asks: *"Would you be willing to go look? I need to know if the old stories are true. And I think you need to know too."*

- 15 silver for the Sleepless contract (from Dalla)
- Deepened relationships with Maren, Dalla, and the townsfolk
- The beginning of a real investigation — notes, maps, rubbings of carvings
- Growing reputation as the people who deal with the strange things
