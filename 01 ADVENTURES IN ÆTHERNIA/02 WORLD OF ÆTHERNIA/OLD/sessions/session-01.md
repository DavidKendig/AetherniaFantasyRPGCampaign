# Session 1: Rot Beneath the Mill

[< Back to Campaign Index](../README.md) | [Next: Session 2](session-02.md)

## Session Overview

**Goal:** Introduce the players to [Millhaven](../locations/millhaven.md), establish the tone, get them working for the [Compact](../factions/wayfarers-compact.md), and deliver the first hint that something is wrong.

**Tone:** Grounded, mundane, with one unsettling thread.

**Estimated Length:** 3-4 hours

---

## Part 1: Arrival in Millhaven

### Setup
The players arrive in Millhaven for their own reasons — work, travel, or a specific contract. How they know each other (or don't) is up to the table.

### Key Beats
- **The Axle & Spoke Inn:** [Brennan Cask](../npcs/key-npcs.md) welcomes them. Introduce the common room, the posting board, and the feel of the town. Let the players settle in.
- **The Posting Board:** Several [Compact contracts](../plot/session-hooks.md) are available. The players can take whichever interests them. But one job is marked as **urgent**: Miller Hesta Dunwood needs help.
- **Meeting the Locals:** Encourage roleplaying. Brennan is chatty. Other locals are in the common room. Establish Millhaven as a place worth caring about.

### GM Notes
- Keep it slow. Let the players breathe. This is a town they'll come back to again and again.
- The inn is cheap: 3 coppers a night, 1 copper for stew and bread.
- If players are Compact members, Brennan recognizes the seal. If not, he explains what the posting board is.

## Part 2: The Mill Job

### The Problem
[Hesta Dunwood](../npcs/key-npcs.md) is furious and frightened. The grain stored in the mill is rotting — but not normally. It started two days ago and is spreading. Entire sacks of wheat are turning black and soft overnight. It smells wrong — not like normal rot. Sweet, almost chemical. And it's spreading faster than any blight she's ever seen.

She's already lost a quarter of her stored grain. If it continues, the surrounding farms will lose their winter supply. The Reeve told her to burn the affected grain. She wants someone to figure out *why* it's happening first.

**Pay:** 10 silver from Hesta, plus the Reeve will add 5 more if the cause is identified.

### Investigation

**What the players can find:**

1. **The rot is unnatural.** Any character with farming, herbalism, or survival knowledge can confirm this isn't any known blight, mold, or pest. The rot spreads in geometric patterns — spirals and branching lines, not random patches.

2. **It started in the foundation.** The rot appears to originate from the lowest level of the mill — the stone foundation near the waterwheel housing. The oldest part of the structure.

3. **The stones are warm.** The foundation stones in the lowest level are noticeably warmer than they should be. Not hot — just *wrong*. Like they absorbed sunlight, except they're underground.

4. **Carvings.** If players examine the warm stones carefully, they find faint carvings beneath centuries of grime. Geometric patterns that match the rot's spread pattern. No one in town can read them. [Maren Blackthorn](../npcs/key-npcs.md) will recognize them as "old marks" — similar to ones in her grandmother's journals — but cannot translate them.

5. **The rot stops at iron.** Grain stored in iron-banded barrels is unaffected. Grain in wooden bins or cloth sacks is rotting. This is a clue about the magical nature of the phenomenon (iron grounds/disrupts raw magical energy — a rule that will become important later).

### Resolution Options
- **Burn the affected grain and move remaining stores to iron containers.** This works as a practical solution. Hesta is unhappy but the crisis is contained.
- **Investigate the carvings.** This leads to Maren Blackthorn and plants a seed for future sessions.
- **Dig into the foundation.** If players are ambitious, they can try to excavate beneath the warm stones. They find only packed earth and older stone beneath — but the warmth goes deeper than they can dig. This is not the Veilstone itself, but it is an old Bright Age structure responding to the weakening Veil.

### There is no combat encounter in this investigation unless the GM adds one (bandits on the road, an aggressive animal, etc). The horror here is *wrongness*, not danger.

## Part 3: Evening in Millhaven

After the mill job, let the players return to the inn. Reward them. Let them talk to NPCs.

### Optional Scenes
- **Maren's Apothecary:** If players bring the carving rubbings or rotted grain to Maren, she studies them with visible unease. She says she needs time to look through her books. (She'll have information in Session 2.)
- **Brother Aldwin:** If players visit the chapel, Aldwin is sympathetic but offers only prayers. He suggests the rot is "the Hearth testing the town's patience." Practically useless, but genuinely kind.
- **Reeve Ashford:** Pays the bonus if the players identified the cause (even partially). He is visibly relieved it's "just old stones" and doesn't want to think about it further.

### End of Session Hook
As the players settle in for the night, mention casually: *several people in town had trouble sleeping last night. Bad dreams. Nothing specific — just a sense of being underground, in the dark, with something vast and pale just out of sight.*

The players may or may not have the same dream. Leave it ambiguous. This leads into [Session 2](session-02.md).
