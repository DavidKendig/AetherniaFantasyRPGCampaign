# The Fraying - A Fantasy RPG Campaign

> *The old world sleeps beneath our feet. Pray it does not wake.*

## Campaign Overview

**Tone:** Begins as low fantasy (grounded, gritty, magic is rare and mistrusted) and slowly escalates into high fantasy as the central crisis unfolds. Players will witness the world *change* around them over the course of the campaign.

**System:** System-neutral. Adapt to your table's preferred ruleset.

**Themes:** Creeping dread, the cost of knowledge, ordinary people facing extraordinary change, the line between salvation and catastrophe.

**Projected Length:** Multi-year campaign. Current material covers the first 3 sessions.
