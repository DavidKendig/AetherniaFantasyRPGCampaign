#### Verse 1
Boots on the cobblestone
Steel on our shoulders
Maps inked in midnight
Names written bolder
Storms on the skyline
Gold in the game
Raise up the standard
Of the Wandering Flame

#### Chorus
Banner of the Wandering Flame (hey!)
