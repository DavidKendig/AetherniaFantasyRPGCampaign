Halflings are a small, bucolic folk who enjoy living in burrows. They prefer villages or farmsteads to towns or cities, and halfling society is comfortable and placid, built around close-knit families, shared meals, and a deep suspicion of anything that disrupts the harvest calendar. Halfling adventurers are rare and often famous in halfling circles; some halflings admire their adventurers, while others think them mad for ever leaving home. Most halflings are quiet on their feet, adept at hiding, and deadly accurate with a bow or sling—skills first honed keeping foxes and worse from the henhouse, and later put to grimmer use when the need arose. Halflings reach adulthood in their early thirties and can live to an age of 145 to 199.
