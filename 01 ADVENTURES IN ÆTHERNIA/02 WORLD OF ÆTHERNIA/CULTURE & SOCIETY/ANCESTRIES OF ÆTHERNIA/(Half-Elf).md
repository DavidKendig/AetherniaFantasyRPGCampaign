Realm - None (Kithkin hold no realm)
Language – Edowan & the tongue of their birth
Deity - Tiān, or that of their Kin parent
Currency – Satoshi & Lyria
Height - 5ft 2in + 3d4 inches
Weight – 120lbs + 6d10 pounds

Half-elves are the children of Kin and Edowan, and in Æthernia they are called the Kithkin—a word first used as an insult, and since claimed with some defiance by those it was meant to wound. They take the height and quickness of their Kin parent and the fine features, upswept ears, and night-sight of their Edowan one, and are unmistakable as either. The Edowan tend to regard them fondly but distantly, as one might a cousin's clever child; the Kin regard them with suspicion, because a Kithkin will still be in their prime when the neighbours who watched them grow up are buried. Neither reaction leaves much room for belonging. As a result the Kithkin hold no realm of their own, though they gather in numbers wherever Edo's enclaves brush against Kin cities—river-ports, embassies, trade-quarters, and the caravan roads between—and there they thrive as translators, factors, envoys, and smugglers, being the rare folk who can pass in both worlds without being wholly at home in either. Kithkin inherit the Edowan resistance to charms and the fey cast of mind that comes with it, but not the long patience; they mature as swiftly as any Kin, reaching adulthood around 20, and can live beyond 180 years—long enough to outlast their Kin families, and nowhere near long enough to be counted among the Edowan.
