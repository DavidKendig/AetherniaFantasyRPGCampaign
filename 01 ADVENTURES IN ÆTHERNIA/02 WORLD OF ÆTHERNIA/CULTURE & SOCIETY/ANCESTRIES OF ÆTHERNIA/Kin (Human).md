The Kin are the most numerous ancestry of Æthernia, as well as the most diverse. They can be found in almost every corner of the world, even in places where their presence is not desired. Lacking the long lifespans of the Kith, Kin compensate with restless ambition, driving them to settle new lands, build sprawling cities, and master every trade they touch. They are the most adaptable of the soulblessed peoples, and many have become the most prosperous merchants, generals, and monarchs the world has known. From the sunlit courts of Illyria to the shadowed halls of the Forbidden Empire, no two Kin cultures are quite alike—yet all share the same restless spirit that carries them wherever fortune, or desperation, leads.

## **Illyrian**
Realm - Illyria
Language – Illyrian
Deities - Soleil & Luné
Currency – Lyria

Illyrians hail from the sunlit realm of Illyria, a land of marble city-states, terraced vineyards, and open-air agoras where philosophy is debated as fiercely as trade. They venerate the twin deities Soleil and Luné, whose eternal chase across the sky is said to govern the turning of day into night, and Illyrian temples are built in matched pairs to honor both. Illyrians prize eloquence, artistry, and civic duty, and their coin, the Lyria, is trusted across half the continent for its uncorrupted silver content.

## **Imperial**
Realm – The Forbidden Empire
Language – Imperial
Deity – Æris
Currency – Dinar

Imperials are the subjects of the Forbidden Empire, a vast and secretive realm whose borders few outsiders are permitted to cross. Bound together by rigid law and the singular worship of Æris, god of order and judgment, Imperials value discipline, hierarchy, and the unquestioned authority of the Empire's magistrates. Their dinar, stamped with the sigil of the reigning magistrate, is accepted everywhere but loved nowhere, a reminder of the Empire's long shadow over Æthernian trade. Those Imperials who leave the Empire's borders are rare, and are watched closely wherever they go.
