Realm - None (nomadic)
Language – Orcish
Deity - Klax
Currency – Plunder
Height: 5ft 6in + 3d4 inches
Weight – 150lbs + 5d10 pounds

The Klaxon are one of the youngest ancestries of Æthernia, and theirs is one of the saddest stories. Born of unions between Kin and orc-kind in the chaos of the wars against the Dark Lord, the Klaxon belong fully to neither people—too orcish for many Kin realms to welcome, too soulblessed for the orc warbands to fully trust. Many have found no realm to call their own, and instead follow the warbands and mercenary companies that will have them, or carve out hard livings on the fringes of Kin and Kith lands alike. What has bound the scattered Klaxon together is the worship of Klax, a war-god born from their suffering, and a currency of convenience they call Plunder rather than coin, since so few of the Klaxon have known a settled home long enough to mint one of their own. For all they have lost, the Klaxon are famed for their endurance in battle and their fierce, unshakeable loyalty to the few who earn it. Klaxon reach adulthood by their late teens and, like their orcish forebears, seldom live past 60 years—a fact that drives many to live with an urgency other ancestries rarely understand.
