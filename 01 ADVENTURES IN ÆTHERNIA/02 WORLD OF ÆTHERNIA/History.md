
The history of Encara is broken up into 3 distinct periods. Each age has a designator as part of its date system to allow readers to instantly know what age of Encaran History they are reading about. While the [Encaran Calendar](Calendar.md) does have, seasons, months, and days, historical scribes have taken to only using numbers to track dates. 0000.001 Æ for instance would be the equivalent of day 1 year 1 of the Ascendant Age.

The period know as the Mythic age (originally was known as the Ancient Age, hence why its dates are marked with Ꜳ) is the prehistoric period of Encaran History. As such, it deviates from the other 2 age's method of tracking time and doesn't actually use dates, instead merely listing events in the generally accepted chronological order.

The Second Age (originally known as the Celestial Age, Œ)

The Third/Current Age, more appropriately titled the Ascendant Epoch or Æ, is the current age of Encara.
