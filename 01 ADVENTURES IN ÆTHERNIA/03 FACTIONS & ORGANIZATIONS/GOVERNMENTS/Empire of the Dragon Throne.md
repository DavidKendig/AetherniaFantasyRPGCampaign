## The Landsråd

Formed by Emperor Léo in 0176 Æ, the Landsråd serves as the legislative body of the Empire. It comprises of one representative from each of the Twelve Realms and reserves a 13th voting seat to be filled by a representative of the guilds. Once little more than a rubber stamp for Imperial decrees, the Landsråd effectively seized power in the wake of the Emperor's Heir's untimely death in 0200 Æ, ensuring another succession crisis would not threaten the realm again.

The Landsråd now elects the Emperor for a life term upon the passing or resignation of the former. While not a written rule and is a practical holdover from their first election, the Landsråd does not nominate one of their own but scourers the land for the appropriate noble lord. The Emperor no longer attends sessions but they hold general veto power that can be overridden by 10 votes and a single-use per year super veto that cannot be overturned.

A quorum of at least 9 seats must be present for a vote to be valid. Simple majority (7 votes) is required for routine matters but a 2/3 majority (9 votes) is required for the appointment of Emperor or declarations of war.

  Special Powers:
  - Right of Petition: Any citizen who gathers 10,000 signatures can force the Landsråd to debate a proposal
  - Censure Authority: Can censure Imperial appointees (governors, generals) with 8 votes, forcing the Emperor to respond publicly
  - The Purse Strings: Must approve annual imperial taxation levels; Emperor cannot raise taxes without consent
  - Treaty Ratification: All foreign treaties require Landsråd approval within 60 days

  Internal Hierarchy:
  - The First Speaker: Elected annually by the body, presides over sessions, sets agenda (cannot be from previous year's realm)
  - The Keeper of Records: Permanent position, maintains all legislative history, considered politically neutral
  - Committee of Three: Rotating mini-council that can convene emergency sessions or investigate corruption

  Limitations & Checks:
  - No Army Command: Cannot directly command military forces (remains Emperor's domain)
  - Imperial Prerogative: Emperor retains sole authority over knighthoods, noble titles, and appointment of judges
  - Sacred Exception: Cannot legislate on matters deemed religious by the Imperial Cult
  - Sunset Clause: All laws expire after 50 years unless renewed (prevents outdated legislation)

  Procedural Traditions:
  - The Empty Chair: A 14th symbolic seat remains vacant, representing "the people's voice" - any representative may speak from it to argue against their own realm's interests
  - Right of Challenge: Once per session, any representative may demand another prove they still hold their realm's confidence (requires written confirmation within 30 days)
  - The Long Debate: Once per decade, the Landsråd may call a 7-day continuous session where all laws can be questioned and reformed

  Conflict Resolution:
  - Inter-Realm Disputes: The Landsråd serves as arbiter when two realms have conflicts; disputing realms cannot vote on their own case
  - Impeachment Power: Can impeach the Emperor with unanimous vote (all 13), triggering a Grand Council of all nobility
