Hear me, brave adventurer, for I have little time. I am the Wizard Mentor, and I call to you across the great Myst that lies between our worlds.

For centuries good and evil were held in balance by the just rule of the Kingdoms of Kith and Kin. That balance was broken when the Dark Lord raised his Dark Tower and delved into the Wyrd arts…

Our prophecies called forth many heroes to oppose the Dark Lord. After many hard campaigns, they vanquished the Dark Lord, but the Tower could not be destroyed and stands to this day.

Now something new stirs beneath it. Our prophecies have fallen silent, and no heir of our heroes still lives. So I have committed this forbidden act: I have reached across the wide sea of the Myst and opened this door to you.

You are not what I expected. You may not be what I hoped for. You will have to be enough.

The door is closing. Take my hand, adventurer, or do not. But decide quickly, and know that it may not open twice.
