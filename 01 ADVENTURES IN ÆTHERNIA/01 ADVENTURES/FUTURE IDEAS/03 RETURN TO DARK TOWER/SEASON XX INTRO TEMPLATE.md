Long ago, the sorcerer-king Ainz Ooal Gown built a Tower in the center of the Four Kingdoms. Behind its ebon walls, he practiced dark sorcery in his search for power. As the dark lord's madness grew, his own apprentices plotted against him. They stole his wondrous treasures and fled the Tower, then recruited heroes from the Four Kingdoms to stop him. One hero prevailed. Ainz Ooal Gown was defeated, and the Tower lay vacant. But now a new adversary has taken up residence. It grows in power, spreading corruption and calling forth wicked creatures from afar. This foe cannot be defeated alone. The heroes of the Four Kingdoms must unite and return to the Tower.
