Company Charter and Articles of Association

Governing the Adventuring Company Known As

**[COMPANY NAME]**

Preamble

We, the undersigned, being adventurers of sound mind and able body, do hereby enter into this solemn compact and agreement for the purpose of forming a chartered adventuring company. We acknowledge the dangers inherent to our profession and the necessity of clear governance to prevent disputes that might endanger our lives and fortunes. This Charter shall bind us in fellowship and regulate our conduct toward one another.

1. Formation and Purpose

The Company is hereby established for the following purposes: the exploration of dungeons, ruins, and other hostile territories; the recovery of treasure and artifacts; the elimination of monsters and threats to civilized lands; the fulfillment of contracts and quests; and all such other adventuring activities as may be undertaken by mutual consent of the members.

2. Membership and Shares

a) **Full Members:** Each signatory to this Charter is a full member entitled to one equal share of all profits, loot, and rewards earned by the Company.

b) **New Members:** New members may be admitted by unanimous consent of existing members. Upon admission, they shall receive a full share equal to all other members.

c) **Hirelings and Retainers:** Non-member hirelings, guides, porters, and specialists shall be paid fixed wages as agreed upon prior to any expedition. They hold no share in profits and have no vote in Company matters.

d) **Departure:** Any member may depart the Company with two weeks' notice. Upon departure, they forfeit all claims to Company property, ongoing contracts, and future earnings, but retain their share of treasure already divided.

3. Leadership and Decision-Making

a) **Company Leader:** The Company shall elect a Leader by majority vote, who shall serve until they resign, are replaced by majority vote, or depart the Company. The Leader shall speak for the Company in negotiations, maintain the Company charter, and organize expeditions.

b) **Major Decisions:** Decisions regarding admission of new members, dissolution of the Company, changes to this Charter, or acceptance of major contracts require unanimous consent.

c) **Routine Decisions:** Tactical decisions during expeditions, minor expenditures, and day-to-day matters require only majority vote. In case of tied votes, the Leader shall cast the deciding vote.

d) **Emergency Authority:** In situations of immediate danger where consultation is impossible, any member may take necessary action to preserve life and limb without prior vote.

4. Division of Treasure and Rewards

a) **Equal Shares:** After payment of Imperial tithes, hireling wages, and Company expenses, all treasure and monetary rewards shall be divided into equal shares among members.

b) **Magic Items:** Magical weapons, armor, and items shall be distributed by consensus based on utility to the Company. Members who can best employ such items shall receive them. If consensus cannot be reached, items shall be sold and proceeds divided.

c) **Company Fund:** The Company shall maintain a treasury equal to 10% of all earnings to cover shared expenses including healing, resurrection, equipment repair, and hireling wages. This fund may be drawn upon by majority vote.

d) **Individual Finds:** Small personal items found on bodies the individual member personally slew, or discovered through individual effort apart from Company activities, belong to that member alone.

5. Death and Incapacitation

a) **Death in Service:** If a member dies during Company activities, their designated heir or estate shall receive their full share from the current expedition. The Company shall make reasonable efforts to recover the body and personal effects.

b) **Resurrection:** The Company shall fund the resurrection of any fallen member up to a cost of 1,000 gold crowns per member. Costs exceeding this amount shall be borne by the individual or their estate.

c) **Permanent Incapacitation:** A member rendered permanently unable to adventure through injury, curse, or other affliction may retire with their current share and a one-time separation payment of 500 gold crowns from the Company fund.

d) **Equipment of the Fallen:** Personal equipment and magic items of deceased members belong to their heirs. However, items critical to Company survival may be retained by majority vote upon payment of fair market value to the heir.


6. Duties and Obligations

a) **Good Faith:** All members shall act in good faith toward one another, share information relevant to Company activities, and refrain from deception or betrayal of fellow members.

b) **Mutual Defense:** Members shall defend one another in combat and shall not abandon companions to death or capture without exhausting all reasonable means of rescue.

c) **Shared Risks:** No member shall be compelled to undertake unreasonable risks, but all shall bear equal danger when danger is unavoidable. Members who consistently refuse to share in dangers may be expelled by unanimous vote of remaining members.

d) **Confidentiality:** Company business, discoveries, and financial matters shall be kept confidential. Members shall not disclose sensitive information to outsiders without Company consent.

7. Disputes and Discipline

a) **Internal Resolution:** Disputes between members shall first be resolved through Company mediation. The Leader shall hear both parties and propose a solution, subject to majority vote.

b) **Arbitration:** If internal resolution fails, disputes may be submitted to binding arbitration by a neutral party agreed upon by both disputants.

c) **Theft or Betrayal:** Any member who steals from the Company, betrays Company secrets for personal gain, or deliberately endangers fellow members shall be immediately expelled and forfeit all shares and claims. Legal action may be pursued as warranted.

d) **Expulsion:** A member may be expelled for cause by unanimous vote of all other members. Expelled members forfeit future shares but retain their portion of earnings prior to expulsion.

_We, the undersigned, hereby bind ourselves to this Charter and swear to uphold its terms in good faith. May fortune favor our endeavors and may our fellowship endure through all perils._
