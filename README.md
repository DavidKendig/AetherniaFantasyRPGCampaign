# ADVENTURES IN ÆTHERNIA FANTASY RPG CAMPAIGN
<a href="https://davidkendig.info"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DavidKendig/TTRPGCampaigns/refs/heads/main/IMAGES/AdventuresIn%C3%86therniaBanner.png" alt="" /></a>
A D&D / OSR Fantasy RPG setting

This is my personal fantasy campaign world from my years of DMing. I am still trying to collate my notes, I never thought to keep them all together over the years 🤦‍♂️, so please pardon the mess that is my attempt to put this project together in a coherent format for your own use.
